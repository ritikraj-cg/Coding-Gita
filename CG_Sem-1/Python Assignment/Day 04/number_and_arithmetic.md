C. Operator Precedence Practice


**Question - 9**

10 + 5 * 2

Step 1: Multiplication first:

5 * 2 = 10

Step 2: Addition:

10 + 10 = 20

Answer: 20



**Question - 10**

(10 + 5) * 2

Step 1: Parentheses first:

10 + 5 = 15

Step 2: Multiplication:

15 * 2 = 30

Answer: 30

Parentheses change the order of calculation. In Question 9, multiplication happens before addition, while here the addition inside parentheses happens first.

**Question - 16**

20 - 5 + 2

Addition and subtraction have the same precedence, so they are calculated from left to right.

20 - 5 = 15

15 + 2 = 17

Answer: 17

Now:

20 - (5 + 2)

Parentheses are calculated first:

5 + 2 = 7

20 - 7 = 13

Answer: 13

The results are different because parentheses change the order of calculation.
